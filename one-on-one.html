Exceed Real Estate · Teruo's Field Guide

Agent 1:1 Conversation Map エージェント1on1 会話マップ

A 60–75 minute structured conversation with each agent. The goal is not evaluation. It's understanding — their work, their goals, their worries, and what they need from the company and from you personally.

Duration 60–75 min
Cadence Quarterly minimum
Setting Private room, phones away, take notes by hand
Confidentiality What's said stays between you (unless safety/legal)
Opening Their Work Wins & Strengths Challenges Concerns about the Company Goals Support Needed Feedback for Teruo Close & Commit

Do — お願いしたい姿勢

  • Listen 70% / talk 30%. Use silence — count to 10 before filling a pause.
  • Take notes by hand. It signals their words matter.
  • Ask "tell me more" instead of jumping to solutions.
  • End with written commitments — yours and theirs.
  • Send a recap email within 24 hours.

Don't — 避けたいこと

  • Don't defend the company or yourself. Just hear it.
  • Don't compare agents ("X is doing better than you").
  • Don't problem-solve on the spot for big issues — say "let me think and come back to you."
  • Don't break confidentiality, even casually.
  • Don't turn it into a sales review. Numbers come later.

1Opening — 入り口

Q1
How are you doing — and I mean outside of work too. Family, health, life in Dubai.
最近どうですか？仕事以外も含めて — ご家族、健康、ドバイでの生活など。
What to listen forEnergy level. Are they tired, stressed, lit up? Real-estate burnout is silent. If they say "fine" too fast, ask once more: "really?"
Q2
Before we get into work — is there anything on your mind that we should address first so the rest of this conversation can be honest?
本題に入る前に、まず話しておきたいことはありますか？それを済ませてから残りの話を率直にしたいので。
Why this question mattersGives them permission to bring up the elephant before you've spent 40 minutes on safer topics. Often the most valuable answer of the whole meeting comes here.

2Their Work — 今の仕事

Q3
Walk me through what you're working on right now. Which deals feel most alive?
今取り組んでいる案件を教えてください。一番「動いている」と感じる案件はどれですか？
What to listen forDon't just hear the pipeline — hear which deals excite them vs. which they're forcing. Mismatch between effort and energy is the first sign of a stalled agent.
Q4
What part of the job energizes you most? And what part drains you?
仕事の中で一番やりがいを感じる瞬間は？逆に、一番消耗するのはどんな時ですか？
Why this mattersEnergy patterns predict retention better than commission numbers. Drain areas tell you where to invest in tooling, admin support, or training. Match the agent's strength to the deal type going forward.
Q5
In a normal week, how much of your time goes to selling vs. paperwork, follow-ups, and admin?
普通の1週間で、実際に「売る」時間と、書類・追客・事務にかける時間の比率はどれくらいですか？
Real-estate specificIf admin is >40% of their week, that's a process problem we can fix — not their fault. Note it for the Exceed Portal Tier 3 roadmap.

3Wins & Strengths — 成果と強み

Q6
Tell me about a recent win — big or small — you're genuinely proud of.
最近の成果で、誇りに思っているものを教えてください。大小は問いません。
Why this mattersMake them say it out loud. Builds confidence. Also gives you material to celebrate publicly in the next team meeting (with their permission).
Q7
What do you think you're better at than anyone else on the team?
チームの中で「これは自分が一番得意」と思えることは何ですか？
What to listen forPeople almost never say this unprompted. Their answer tells you how to deploy them — who to pair them with, which leads to route to them, where to put them on the website.

4Challenges — 課題

Q8
What's the hardest part of selling in Dubai right now? Specifically this market, this quarter.
今のドバイの市場で、一番難しいと感じる部分はどこですか？特にこの四半期で。
What to listen forMarket intel from the front line. Compare answers across agents — patterns reveal what's actually happening in Dubai vs. what reports say. Cross-reference with IRR Simulator area data.
Q9
What's the single biggest thing blocking you from closing more deals?
もっと成約を増やすために、今一番のボトルネックは何ですか？
What to listen forListen for one of these: lead quantity, lead quality, listing access, paperwork speed, marketing collateral, language support, your time. Resist the urge to defend any of these.
Q10
When was the last time you felt stuck and didn't know who to ask? What happened?
「困ったけど、誰に聞けばいいかわからない」と感じた最近の経験は？その時どうしましたか？
Why this mattersReveals mentorship and process gaps. Don't justify ("you could've asked X") — just write it down. If two agents give the same answer, you have a systemic gap.

5Concerns about the Company — 会社への気がかり

Q11
Is there anything happening in the company right now that worries you? Even a small uncomfortable feeling counts.
今、会社の中で「気になっている」「ちょっと違和感がある」ことはありますか？小さな引っかかりでも結構です。
The most important question of the entire meetingStay silent for at least 10 seconds after they finish. Do not react defensively to anything they say. Take a note, say "thank you for telling me," then ask "tell me more."
Q12
If you could change one thing about how we work as a team, what would it be?
もし、私たちの働き方を一つだけ変えられるとしたら、何を変えますか？
Framing trickPhrased as a gift, not a complaint. People will say things here they wouldn't say if you asked "what's wrong?" Often surfaces the real underlying issue.
Q13
Is there anyone on the team you find it hard to work with — and you don't have to name them if you don't want to?
チームの中で「一緒に働くのが少し難しい」と感じる人はいますか？名前を出さなくても大丈夫です。
Use carefullyOnly ask if rapport is good. If they decline, move on instantly — don't push. If they share, never repeat names. Watch for repeated themes across multiple 1:1s — that's the real signal.

6Goals — 目標

Q14
Where do you want to be 12 months from now? Deals closed, income, role, skills — be concrete.
12ヶ月後、自分がどうなっていたいですか？成約数、収入、役割、スキル — できるだけ具体的に。
Get numbersPush gently for a number. "Around" is fine. Write it down with them — both of you keep a copy. This becomes the anchor for the next 1:1.
Q15
What's one stretch goal you've been a little afraid to say out loud?
「言うのが少し怖いけど、本当はやってみたい」目標はありますか？
Why this mattersPermission to dream. Often reveals leadership aspirations, a specialization (off-plan, ultra-prime, a specific area), or a desire to move into management. Knowing this changes how you invest in them.
Q16
3 years out — do you still see yourself at Exceed? What would have to be true for that to be a "yes"?
3年後も、Exceedで働いている自分を想像できますか？「はい」と言うためには、何が必要ですか？
What to listen forThe "what would have to be true" part is the real answer. It's a soft way of asking "what would make you stay." Note conditions carefully — these are your retention levers.

7Support Needed — 必要なサポート

Q17
What support do you need from the company that you don't have today?
今、会社からもっと欲しいサポートは何ですか？
Common answers in Dubai real estateBetter CRM, more qualified leads, marketing materials in JP/EN/AR, admin support for paperwork, Bayut/PF/Dubizzle credits, video/photo for listings, mortgage broker intros, training on off-plan vs. secondary. Commit to fixing 1–2 things — don't promise everything.
Q18
And what support do you need specifically from me — separate from the company?
会社全体の話とは別に、私（Teruo）個人にサポートしてほしいことは何ですか？
Why split the questionDifferent answer set. Common: more 1:1 time, intros to specific clients, advice on a deal in progress, public recognition, being shielded from a specific stakeholder, more autonomy. Take this personally — it's about your behavior as CEO.

8Feedback for Teruo — 私へのフィードバック

Q19
What's one thing I do as CEO that you'd want me to keep doing?
私のCEOとしての行動で、「これは続けてほしい」と思うことは？
Why start positiveBuilds the trust needed for Q20. Also useful for you — knowing what's working is as valuable as knowing what isn't.
Q20
And one thing you wish I'd do differently — or stop doing entirely?
逆に、「ここはこうしてほしい」「これはやめてほしい」と思うことはありますか？
Hardest question of the meetingSit with the silence. Do not justify. Do not explain why you did it. Just: "thank you, that's helpful — can you give me an example so I understand exactly when this happens?" Take notes.

9Close & Commit — まとめと約束

Q21
Is there anything I didn't ask that you were hoping I would?
私が聞かなかったけれど、本当は聞いてほしかったことはありますか？
Catches the icebergAlways ask this. Sometimes the answer is "no" and you're done. Sometimes it's the most important thing said all hour.
Q22
Before our next 1:1, what's one concrete thing each of us will commit to? One from you, one from me.
次回の1on1までに、お互いに一つずつ約束する具体的なアクションは何にしましょうか？私から一つ、あなたから一つ。
Why mutual commitmentIf only they commit, it feels like homework. If only you commit, it feels like a complaint session. Both → it's a partnership. Write both down on a shared page. Reference them at the start of next 1:1.

After the Meeting — 24時間以内にやること

Within 1 hour
Write down your raw notes while fresh. Star the 2–3 things that surprised you.
Within 24 hours
Send a short recap email in their preferred language. Restate their goal, both commitments, and one specific thing you appreciated.
Within 1 week
Take action on at least one piece of support they asked for. Even small action builds trust faster than a perfect plan.
Across all 1:1s
Keep a private grid: agent × theme. When 3+ agents raise the same concern, that's a company-level issue, not an agent issue. Address it at the team level.
Never
Quote one agent to another. Even casually. Even positively. Even "X also said that." Trust is built in years and broken in one sentence.
